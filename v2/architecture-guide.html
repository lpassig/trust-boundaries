    Mission and decision ownership
    

    The Mission Authority owns the mission ID, issuer, subject, agent, purpose, allowed resource/action constraints, permitted destinations, expiry, budget, parent mission, version and state. Store it outside the agent's editable memory. The business PDP retrieves authoritative attributes through a PIP and returns a bound decision to an authenticated resource PEP. The PEP obtains operation and parameters from the actual request and validates currency of the decision before execution. Vault applies a separate, non-expanding credential decision: delegated OBO uses human baseline ACL, agent ceiling and applicable RAR; autonomous uses its own baseline and applicable constraints. A successful lease cannot authorize unrelated business data.
